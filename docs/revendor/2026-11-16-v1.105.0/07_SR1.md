## The title-bar pass (rulings 3 and 4)

DpsRace draws four windows of its own with title-bar controls. The library's existing surfaces do the
work: `LibKa0s-Tooltip-1.0`'s `PlaceAttached` and `AttachTitleTip`, and Core's `MakeCloseButton` with
the owner. `W.HeaderButtons` is not adopted: the addon's own strip (`modules/Style.lua`) is the smaller
change, three new helpers on it.

- `Style.TitleStrip()`: a strip whose hovered control takes `text.link` (`Style.LINK`); at rest
  `icon.tint` (`Style.REST`), as before. `Style.NewStrip(hot)` keeps the gold for the strips that are
  not on a title bar (the timeline's zoom controls).
- `Style.TitleButton(...)`: `Style.ControlButton` with the title-bar tooltip: its `tip` in
  `gtip.title`, owned by the control at `ANCHOR_NONE`, placed by `PlaceAttached` (above, flips below),
  falling back to `BOTTOM` on the control's `TOP` + 2 where the geometry cannot be read.
- `Style.TitleTip(b, tip)`: the same tooltip hooked onto an existing control. On a library close it
  clears the library's one-line words (`AttachTitleTip(b, false)`) so the host's lines show instead,
  and leaves the take-down to the library's own leave.
- `core/SkinSetup.lua`'s degraded `SKIN_FALLBACK` gains `text.link` (Ka0s Default `{ 1, 0.5, 0, 1 }`).

| Window | Control | Tooltip | Rest | Hover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Race HUD | close | "Close" + what closing does (`HudTips.close`), `TitleTip` | `icon.tint` (`close.rest`) | `close.hot` |
| Race HUD | lock | "Lock or unlock the HUD", `TitleButton` | `icon.tint` | `text.link` (padlock art swaps with the state, its color carries none) |
| Race HUD | settings | "Settings", `TitleButton` | `icon.tint` | `text.link` |
| Race HUD | Results | "Results", `TitleButton` | `icon.tint` | `text.link` |
| Results | close | "Close", the library's `AttachTitleTip` | `icon.tint` (`close.rest`) | `close.hot` |
| Results | view toggle | "Collapse: ..." / "Expand: ...", `TitleButton` | `icon.tint` | `text.link` |
| Ability timeline | close | "Close", the library's | `icon.tint` (`close.rest`) | `close.hot` |
| Gear view | close | "Close", the library's | `icon.tint` (`close.rest`) | `close.hot` |

The controls draw no glow texture of their own (they only ever recolored), so there was no glow to keep.

Tests, red first (`tests/test_title_bar.lua`, new, one case per window under Ka0s Default and Obsidian,
on the kit's paint recorder so the skin's close painter finds its art): every control shows a non-empty
tooltip owned by it, titled in `gtip.title`, above it, taken down on leave; rest and hover resolve to
`icon.tint` / `text.link` (`close.hot` for the X), read from `ResolveSkin`. Against the previous
modules the HUD and Results cases are red (a white `gtip.value` title, the gold hover); the timeline's
and gear view's pass from the re-copy alone. `test_hud_window.lua`'s strip case is re-pinned from the
gold to `text.link`, as the ruling changes that contract.
